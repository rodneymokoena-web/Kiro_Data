    How it works: trades are rebuilt from the tester's deals. Journal logs do not contain money per deal, so profit = price move × lots × the value per lot (guessed from the symbol - check it) minus a cost per lot, which is solved so the total matches the log's final balance (spread, commission, swap). Logs from mt5_tester_log.py and HTML reports carry their own profit. R = profit / money at risk at the initial stop (journal) or price move / stop distance (report), where the stop is known. Partial closes of one position count as one trade (exit marked *, size-weighted; reason e.g. Partial+BE = part closed at the first target, rest at breakeven) unless the box is unticked. Breakeven = stopped at the entry (within the tolerance). Win rate counts breakeven trades as neither wins nor losses in “excl. BE”.
